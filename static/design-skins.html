Moonglade skins — five palettes, one chrome

The same sample chrome under every skin at once. Skins are achievement-unlocked palette swaps applied as <html data-skin>; a skin overrides the meaningful subset of tokens and everything else inherits the base palette. The columns below are built by reading the skin rules out of this page's stamped stylesheet and re-scoping each one to its column — add a skin to DESIGN_TOKENS_CSS and it grows a column here on the next export, with no edit to this page.
