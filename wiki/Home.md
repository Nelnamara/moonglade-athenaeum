## Help inside the app

Everything on this wiki is also in the app. Press **?** on the keyboard (outside a text field), or
click the round **?** button — in the header of the gallery, the Generate dock, the Folio and the
Control Panel, at the bottom right of The Loom, or on a phone in the icon row at the top — and the
**Guide** opens over whatever you were doing, on the page for that screen. **Esc** closes it.

- **The page list** runs down the left, with **Search the guide** above it: type a few letters and
  it lists the pages and the headings that match; **↑ ↓** and **Enter** pick one. The command
  palette (**Ctrl K**, or **⌘ K**) reaches the same pages: **Open the guide**, or start typing a
  page or heading.
- **Back ‹ and forward ›**, and a breadcrumb, move you between pages you have opened. A link to
  another page opens it in place; a link to the outside world opens in a new tab and is marked **↗**.
- **Underlined words** are terms from the [Glossary](Glossary): hover one (on a phone, tap it) for
  what it means, and **Glossary ›** goes to its entry.
- **The pages are the ones that came with your install**, so they describe the version you are
  running. When a newer release's copy of the page differs, a line at the foot says **A newer
  version of this page is online ↗**.
- **About** is the last page in the list: the version, the art pack, and what changed in this release.
  It is also reached from the Control Panel's version stamp, and on a phone from **Control → About
  Moonglade**. See [Control Panel → Updates and About](Control-Panel#updates-and-about).
- **On a phone** the guide is a full-height sheet: **‹** goes back (or closes), and **☰** shows the
  page list.

### The first-run guide

The first time you open a screen, a small **welcome card** says what it is for — the gallery
(**Welcome to the stacks**), the Generate dock, The Loom, the Folio and the Control Panel each have
one. It has two buttons:

- **Show me around** (on a phone, **Show me**) starts a short tour: three or four marks pointing at
  the real controls, each with **Next**, **Skip tour** (on a phone, **Skip**) and, on the last,
  **Done**.
- **Got it** closes the card and ends the guide for that screen.

**Skip tour** (or **Esc**) also ends it for that screen. Finishing the tour with **Done** is the one
way into **Nel's notes**: one small note at a time, pinned beside a control, and the next one
appears after you have used that control. A note steps aside for dialogs and never covers the
header's buttons; **got it** dismisses one, **hide notes** turns all of them off, and **Esc** waves
off the one on screen.

Nothing is written until you answer a card, and your answers are saved to your account, so the
guide does not come back on another device. The Guide's left column keeps three buttons for it:
**Replay this tour** (for the screen you opened the Guide from), **Hide Nel's notes** / **Show
Nel's notes**, and **Reset guides**, which puts every screen back to its first visit.
